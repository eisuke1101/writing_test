6Write about TV and movies.（テレビ・映画）

生活

Maybe you could write about（想定される話題）
Which do you prefer, and why?
TV you usually watch in the morning/evening
Movies as a way of learning languages
Note this point（学習ポイント）
“interesting”は、通常、知的な内容について用いられます（映画については、用いられません）。
Examples:
I watched a program about British history. It was interesting.
I watched a Brad Pitt movie. It was really interesting good!
Here’s an example（ネイティヴが書いた英語日記例）

Online services provide some really good movie and TV content these days. I subscribe to one of the major services, and it has a wide range of great movies and interesting documentaries.

Recently I watched Parasite, which won the Oscar for best movie this year. It was fantastic. I also watched some of the other Oscar nominee movies, which were excellent too.

The Oscar documentaries were also interesting. I saw one called American Factory, which was about cultures colliding. I wish I had more time to watch all these good movies and interesting documentaries!
